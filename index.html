Adventures in Fire Island

We wound along a narrow mountain road, the air sharp and clean, as snow-capped peaks loomed above us like sleeping giants. Around one bend, a herd of elk grazed in a meadow dotted with wildflowers, and higher up, a bald eagle traced slow circles against the sky. We stopped by a glacial lake so still it mirrored the mountains perfectly, and just before dusk, a red fox darted across the trail, pausing only long enough to glance back at us before vanishing into the pines.

I spent a peaceful afternoon at the park, where winding trails led through open meadows and shaded groves of old trees. A calm lake at the center reflected the sky, and ducks drifted lazily near the shore. I spotted squirrels darting along the paths and heard birdsong throughout the walk, while benches along the ridge offered sweeping views of the surrounding hills.
